Enterprise Cloud Migration

Government Enterprise &bull; Government

Project Overview

About the Project

A comprehensive cloud transformation project involving the migration of 200+ servers, 50TB of data, and 30+ enterprise applications to a hybrid cloud environment while maintaining zero downtime during business hours.

The Challenge

The client operated aging on-premises infrastructure with significant maintenance costs, limited scalability, and no disaster recovery capability. The challenge was to migrate to cloud while maintaining 24/7 availability of critical government services.

Our Solution

Implemented a phased hybrid cloud migration strategy using Microsoft Azure, establishing dedicated connectivity, migrating workloads in prioritized batches, and establishing comprehensive monitoring and disaster recovery.

Key Results

40% reduction in infrastructure costs
99.99% uptime achieved
Disaster recovery RTO reduced from 48 hours to 2 hours
Full migration completed in 6 months with zero downtime
