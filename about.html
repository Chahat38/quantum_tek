Who We Are

A Technology Partner You Can Trust

Quantum Tech Solutionz is a technology solutions provider offering end-to-end digital services including web development, mobile apps, cloud computing, AI, cybersecurity, and infrastructure solutions. The company positions itself as a system integrator with nationwide reach in Pakistan. Founded by Kamran Rashid TI (M) (Founder & CEO), we bring a unique combination of military precision, technical excellence, and business acumen to every engagement.

We serve government agencies, enterprise corporations, telecom operators, defense organizations, and private sector clients across Pakistan — delivering secure, scalable, and future-ready technology infrastructure.

Our team of developers, designers, and security experts turns ideas into powerful digital solutions.
