Smart Campus ICT Infrastructure

Educational Institution &bull; Education

Project Overview

About the Project

Greenfield ICT infrastructure project covering structured cabling, campus-wide Wi-Fi, 1,000+ IP cameras, CCTV management systems, video conferencing rooms, and a centralized network operations center.

The Challenge

The institution required a modern, integrated ICT infrastructure to support 15,000 students and 2,000 staff with reliable connectivity, security, and collaboration capabilities across a large multi-building campus.

Our Solution

Deployed enterprise-grade Cisco networking infrastructure, Axis IP camera systems, Cisco video conferencing, and a fully managed NOC with 24/7 monitoring and support.

Key Results

100% campus Wi-Fi coverage achieved
1,000+ IP cameras deployed and operational
Network uptime of 99.95%
Full deployment completed in 8 months
